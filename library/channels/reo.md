# Channel: REO

## Identity
- Display name (speech bubble): `REO`
- Character (sticker pack): `reo`
- Language of the videos: French
- Default set: `reo-office` (the illustrated office); `cozy-desk` / `cozy-desk-night` as coded variants

## Look
- Decor: the illustrated home office (`reo-office`), sunset. Other scenes only when explicitly asked.
- Cosy pixel look with clean visuals: no game-like UI (quests, XP bars, achievements).
- Typography `modern` (titles in bold sans); the pixel font only for the speaker tag.

## Rhythm and restraint
- The video takes its time: change shot when the idea changes, not at every sentence (about 9-10 shots per minute at most for a balanced pace).
- The speaker is on screen most of the time; illustrations only where they show what the voice cannot.
- No terminal or code window unless the speaker talks about code or commands. Never invent commands, ticket numbers, tools or figures.
- At most ~4 sweeps/whips/fades per minute; cuts otherwise.

## Sound
- Lofi jazz background music (`lofi-jazz-retro-coffee-shop`), volume about 0.3, ducked under the voice.
- Sound effects on.

## Visual shots
- Text and illustration shots on the `symbols` backdrop (episode colours, faint pixel icons), the speaker in the picture-in-picture.

## Captions
- Bubble when the speaker is on screen talking, classic subtitles over illustrations, none on full-screen text.

## Character
- `reo` uses a pixel-art sticker pack (15 emotions, including laughing, confused, proud, serious, wink, sad, angry). Use the full range: it is what makes the character feel alive.
